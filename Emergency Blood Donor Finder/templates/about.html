About Blood Finder

What is Blood Finder?

Technology with a human purpose.

Blood Finder is a simple emergency donor directory designed to shorten the distance between a person in need and a willing local donor.

The system works in three steps: donors register their name, blood group, phone, and city; a patient or helper searches using the needed group and location; a matching donor can then be contacted directly.

A reliable blood supply begins with people who choose to show up for one another.
01

Why it matters

Blood cannot be manufactured. Donations are essential for treatment, surgery, and emergency care.

02

How it helps

A focused city and group search turns a stressful moment into a clear next step.

03

The benefit

More visible local donors can mean faster coordination for families and care teams.
